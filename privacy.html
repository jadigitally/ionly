Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page covers the Ionly website only — not the Ionly desktop application itself, which has no network access and collects nothing (see the app's own documentation for how it handles your files).

What we collect

If you use the "Notify me at launch" form, we collect the email address you enter. We use it to:

  • let you know when Ionly is ready to download, and
  • send occasional notices about important security or software updates after you've started using Ionly, if you choose to stay subscribed.

We don't collect anything else through this form, and we don't ask for or need your name or any other personal detail to do this.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address for as long as you stay subscribed — so we can reach you both for the launch announcement and for any later security update notices. You can unsubscribe at any time, which stops all future emails and removes your address from our list.

Website analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how many people visit this site — pages viewed, referring sites, and approximate country. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint or individually identify visitors, and is not combined with the email list above. See Cloudflare's own page on Web Analytics for how it works.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies. We don't use advertising trackers, session cookies, or cross-site tracking of any kind. If that ever changes (for example, if we switch to an analytics tool that relies on cookies), we will add a cookie consent banner and update this section before doing so, as required by law.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the email address we hold for you at any time, and you can unsubscribe at any time (every email we send will include an unsubscribe link). To make a request, contact us at info@jadigital.fi.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

This policy was drafted as a plain-language starting point and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Review and adjust it — especially the analytics and email-provider details above — before relying on it for real visitors.
